## meticulous /məˈtɪkjələs/
- adj. showing great attention to detail; very careful and precise
  - She kept meticulous records of every transaction.
  - His meticulous preparation impressed the committee.
- adv. (meticulously) in a way that shows great attention to detail
  - The crime scene was meticulously documented.

> Note: Collocates: meticulous records / planning / attention.
> Dung nhieu trong van viet formal (IELTS Writing Task 2).
> Marks: favorite
> Tags: adjectives

## resilience /rɪˈzɪliəns/
- noun. the capacity to recover quickly from difficulties
  - The team showed remarkable resilience after the setback.
  - Economic resilience depends on diversification.

> Marks: difficult

## give up
- phrasal-verb. to stop trying; to quit
  - She decided to give up smoking.
- phrasal-verb. to surrender something to someone else
  - He refused to give up his seat on the crowded train.

> Tags: phrasal-verbs

## take into account
- idiom. to consider something when making a decision
  - You must take into account the cost of maintenance.

> Note: Bien the: take sth into account / take account of sth.
> Tags: idioms

## bottleneck /ˈbɒtlnek/
- noun. a point of congestion that slows progress
  - The single lane bridge is a major bottleneck during rush hour.
- verb. to slow down by creating congestion
  - Requests are bottlenecked by the legacy database.

> Tags: technology
