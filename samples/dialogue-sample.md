## dialogue
> Title: Đặt bàn nhà hàng
> Speakers: Nhân viên, Khách

- Nhân viên: Good evening! Do you have a reservation?
- Khách: {{Yes, under the name Hung, for two people.}}
- Nhân viên: Perfect. Would you like still or sparkling water?
- Khách: {{Still water, please.}}
- Nhân viên: Are you ready to order, or do you need a few more minutes?
- Khách: {{Could we have a few more minutes, please?}}

> Explanation: "Under the name ..." dùng khi xác nhận đặt chỗ. "Could we have
> a few more minutes" là cách lịch sự để xin thêm thời gian.

## dialogue
> Title: Hỏi đường
> Speakers: Khách du lịch, Người dân

- Khách du lịch: Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to the train station?
- Người dân: {{Sure. Go straight for two blocks, then turn left at the traffic lights.}}
- Khách du lịch: Is it far? Should I take a taxi?
- Người dân: {{It's about a ten-minute walk, so you don't need a taxi.}}

> Explanation: Chú ý cụm chỉ đường: go straight, turn left/right, at the traffic lights.

## dialogue
> Title: Thảo luận nhóm về môi trường
> Speakers: Lan, Minh, Hoa

- Lan: What do you think is the biggest environmental problem in our city?
- Minh: {{For me it's air pollution — there are simply too many motorbikes.}}
- Hoa: {{I'd say waste management. Most rubbish still isn't sorted properly.}}
- Lan: So what could the city do about it?
- Minh: {{They could invest more in public transport to reduce traffic.}}
