## mcq
What does "give up" most nearly mean?

- [ ] to donate something
- [x] to stop trying
- [ ] to increase gradually
- [ ] to arrive late

> Explanation: "Give up" means to quit or abandon an effort.

## mcq
Choose the correct sentence.

- [x] She takes after her mother.
- [ ] She takes her mother after.
- [ ] She takes after of her mother.

> Explanation: "Take after somebody" khong tach roi va khong dung gioi tu "of".
